GradED — a protocol for reproducible, expert-attested competence certification on a blockchain.

The idea

Turning independently accumulated knowledge into a recognized credential — the way a degree does, but reproducible and expert-attested.

The problem

Anyone can now learn a field independently, but there's no way to convert accumulated knowledge into a recognized credential the way a university degree does — especially for an established professional proving competence in an adjacent field.

The idea

GradED is a protocol for reproducible, expert-attested competence certification. A specialist submits a reproducible work artifact (hashed on-chain); qualified domain experts sign granular, item-level attestations against a shared rubric; when weighted expert support passes a threshold, the protocol issues a certificate.

What makes it different

It is not "a diploma as an NFT". It combines:

  • A Merkle-committed rubric
  • Item-level attestations
  • Domain-scoped reputation
  • Weighted expert support
  • Cryptographic accountability for reviewers
  • Chain-derived reputation
  • Evidence-based slashing
  • A protocol-issued certificate

Three roles kept distinct

Competence reputation

A subject's skill.

Attester credibility

The weight of an expert's vote.

Consensus authority

The right to produce and finalize blocks.

Competence can grow into consensus authority only through quorum-approved, rate-limited on-chain promotion.

Honest limitations

Blockchain makes expert actions transparent and verifiable — it does not replace institutions, solve who the trusted experts fundamentally are, or judge whether an honest review is correct.
